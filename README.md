## Director: AI video studio

[`director/`](director/) is an AI video studio. Upload images, footage, audio, documents or a script, and an AI director plans the storyboard, writes the narration, edits clips, designs motion graphics and renders MP4s with [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes).
