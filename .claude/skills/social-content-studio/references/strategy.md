## 1. Goals → metrics

Pick one primary goal per period, because it decides formats, CTAs and what "good" looks like.

| Goal | Primary metrics | Content emphasis | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness / reach** | Reach, non-follower reach %, shares/sends | Relatable, entertaining, broad-but-niche, trend formats | "Send this to…" |
| **Audience growth** | Follows per reach, profile visits | Series, strong promise, personality | "Follow for part 2" |
| **Engagement / community** | Comments, saves, DMs, story replies | Opinions, questions, education, reply videos | "Tell me…" / "Save this" |
| **Leads** | DMs, link clicks, sign-ups | Lead magnets, frameworks, case studies | "Comment GUIDE" / link in bio |
| **Sales** | Clicks, conversions, revenue, code uses | Demos, UGC, testimonials, offers, FAQs | "Shop via link" / code |

Make goals SMART: "Grow Instagram from 2k to 5k followers in 90 days with 4 Reels and 1 carousel a week", not "grow".

## 2. The content funnel

| Stage | Purpose | Formats | Signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TOFU** (top: discovery) | Reach strangers | Relatable POV, myth-busting, trends, entertaining education, hot takes | Shares, reach, 3s hold |
| **MOFU** (middle: trust) | Convert viewers to followers who trust you | Tutorials, frameworks, BTS, stories, case studies, reply videos | Saves, follows, comments |
| **BOFU** (bottom: conversion) | Turn followers into customers | Demos, testimonials, offers, FAQs/objections, comparisons, launches | DMs, clicks, sales |

**Mix by phase** (a starting point, not a rule):
- Growth phase: ~60% TOFU / 30% MOFU / 10% BOFU
- Steady state: ~40 / 40 / 20
- Launch window (1–2 weeks): ~30 / 30 / 40

Too much BOFU makes the account an ad channel and people stop watching. Too little means you have an audience but no business.

## 3. Content pillars and mix

**Pillars** are the 3–5 recurring themes the brand covers. They keep ideas flowing, keep the brand coherent, and teach the algorithm (and the audience) what you're about.

How to pick them:
1. List the audience's top problems/desires (from comments, reviews, sales calls, search).
2. List what the brand can credibly talk about (expertise, product, story, values).
3. Pillars are where those two lists overlap. Add one personality/BTS pillar for the human side.
4. Give each pillar a share of output, default formats and a goal.

Example (an independent coffee roaster):
| Pillar | Share | Default formats | Funnel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brew better coffee at home | 35% | Tutorials, mistake→fix, comparisons | TOFU/MOFU |
| Coffee myths & industry truths | 20% | Hot takes, myth vs fact, green-screen | TOFU |
| Behind the roastery | 20% | Process BTS, day-in-the-life, ASMR | MOFU |
| Community & customers | 15% | Reply videos, UGC, customer stories | MOFU |
| Beans & offers | 10% | Tasting notes, new-release demos, subscriptions | BOFU |

**Content matrix:** cross pillars with formats to generate ideas fast. For example, "Brew better" × "Comparison" gives "$20 vs $200 grinder, blind taste test", and "Myths" × "POV" gives "POV: someone tells you dark roast has more caffeine".

## 4. The idea engine

Never run out of ideas by keeping a running **idea bank** (a doc or the calendar CSV's backlog) fed from these sources:
- **Questions:** comments, DMs, FAQs, sales calls, support tickets, Reddit/Quora, the "People also ask" box in Google, the TikTok search bar's autocomplete.
- **Objections:** every reason people don't buy is a video.
- **Mistakes:** what beginners get wrong, what you got wrong.
- **Myths:** what the niche believes that isn't true.
- **Stories:** customer wins, failures, origin story, weird days.
- **Processes:** how the thing is made or done.
- **Comparisons:** X vs Y, cheap vs expensive, beginner vs pro.
- **Opinions:** hot takes and predictions.
- **News/trends:** in the niche, with your angle added.
- **Winners:** your top 10% of posts. Remake them with a new hook, format or angle.
- **Competitors' winners:** study why they worked and adapt the angle, never copy.

**Idea table format** (use when presenting ideas):
| # | Working title | Hook (spoken) | Format | Pillar | Funnel | Why it'll be shared/saved |

## 5. Cadence

Consistency beats volume. A pace the team can keep for 6 months beats a burst followed by silence. Buffer's 2026 engagement data points the same way. Starting points:

| Platform | Sustainable | Growth push |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 3/week | 5–7/week |
| Instagram carousels | 1/week | 2–3/week |
| Instagram Stories | 3–5 days/week | Daily |
| TikTok | 3–5/week | 1–3/day |
| YouTube Shorts | 3/week | Daily |
| LinkedIn | 2–3/week | 5/week |

Posting time matters less than the content. Post when the audience is active (check insights), then test. The first hour's engagement helps, so be available to reply.

## 6. Weekly production workflow (batching)

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 1. Plan (1h) | Review last week's analytics → pick 4–8 ideas from the bank → assign pillar/format/goal |
| 2. Script (2–3h) | Hooks first, then scripts in the template, then a timing check, shot lists and B-roll lists |
| 3. Film (2–4h) | Batch by setup, hooks 2–3 ways, plenty of B-roll, change outfits between videos |
| 4. Edit (3–6h) | Rough cut → retention pass (cut dead air, add pattern breaks) → captions → sound → export |
| 5. Schedule + write captions | Captions with keywords, hashtags, covers, schedule, prepare Stories |
| Daily (20–30 min) | Reply to comments/DMs, especially in the first hour after posting; engage in the niche |

## 7. Content calendar

Use `assets/content-calendar-template.csv`. Columns:
`date, platform, pillar, funnel_stage, format, working_title, hook, cta, goal_metric, status, owner, asset_link, notes`

Status flow: `idea → scripted → filmed → edited → scheduled → posted → reviewed`.

Calendar tips:
- Map series to fixed days ("Myth Monday"). Fixed days build audience habit and make planning easier.
- Put launches, holidays and industry events on the calendar first, then fill around them.
- Leave 1–2 open slots a week for trends and reactive content.
- After posting, record the key numbers in the notes column so the next plan learns from them.

## 8. Repurposing (1 → 3 → 10)

One deep piece of content (a podcast, webinar, long YouTube video, blog post or customer interview) can feed weeks of social:

1. **One** anchor piece.
2. **Three** core derivatives: vertical clips, text posts (insights, frameworks, contrarian takes) and visual stills (carousels, quote cards).
3. **Ten or more** platform-native variations.

Rules:
- **Re-edit, don't just crop.** Each clip needs its own hook, a reframe to 9:16, captions and a self-contained payoff. Pick moments with a complete thought, a strong opinion, a story or a surprising fact.
- **Native per platform:** different length, text, caption and CTA. No watermarks from other platforms.
- **Don't repost the same video as new on the same platform.** Instagram suppresses near-duplicates. Remake it with a new hook and edit instead.
- **Long-form ↔ short-form loop:** Shorts link to the full video (YouTube "related video"), clips promote the podcast, carousels summarise the webinar, and the newsletter expands on what the clips couldn't fit.

## 9. Trends

- **Adapt the format, keep your niche.** A trend is a structure (audio, edit pattern, meme template) waiting for your topic.
- **Speed matters.** Trends peak within days. Keep 1–2 slots open each week.
- **Check that the trend fits the brand voice** and that the audio is licensed for the account type.
- **Evergreen > trends** for the long-term library. Trends are seasoning, not the meal.

## 10. Collaborations, creators and UGC

- **Collab posts** (Instagram): co-author posts with a complementary creator or brand, so the post reaches both audiences.
- **Creator partnerships:** micro- and nano-creators (roughly 1k–100k followers) often bring higher trust and engagement per dollar than big names. Brief them well (see the creator brief in `script-formats.md`) and let them speak in their own voice.
- **UGC creators:** pay for content (not their audience) to run on your accounts or as ads. Get usage rights in writing.
- **Customer UGC:** encourage it with a branded hashtag, packaging inserts, or giveaways (within rules). Always ask permission and credit.
- **Disclosure:** every paid or gifted relationship needs clear disclosure (see `compliance.md`).

## 11. Paid amplification

- **Boost winners, not hopes.** Put spend behind organic posts that already beat the account's median shares and retention (Spark Ads on TikTok, partnership ads or boosted Reels on Meta).
- **Creative is the targeting.** On modern ad systems the content decides who sees it. Test 3–5 hook variants of the same body.
- **Keep it native:** ads that look like posts beat ads that look like ads.

## 12. 30-day plan for a new or restarting account

**Week 1, foundation:** fill in the brand brief, optimise the profile (name field, bio, link, highlights), pick 3–5 pillars and 2 series, write 15–20 hooks, script the first 6–8 videos.
**Week 2, publish and learn:** post 4–5 Reels/TikToks across all pillars and 1 carousel. Reply to every comment. Note 3s hold and average watch % for each.
**Week 3, double down:** identify the top 2 posts. Make 3 variations of each (new hooks, the same idea, a different format). Keep testing one new format.
**Week 4, systemise:** settle on the cadence, batch-film week 5–6, pin the 3 best posts, add the first BOFU post (offer/lead magnet), and do a first analytics review using `analytics.md`.
