## 1. How every recommendation feed works

Every algorithmic feed runs roughly the same loop:

1. **Classify.** The platform reads the caption, on-screen text, speech (auto-transcribed), audio, visuals and hashtags to work out what the post is about and who might want it. Clear topic signals → better test audience.
2. **Test.** It shows the post to a small seed audience: some followers plus people interested in the topic.
3. **Measure.** It checks whether people stopped scrolling, how long they watched, whether they rewatched, shared, saved, commented or followed, and whether they swiped away fast or tapped "not interested".
4. **Expand or stop.** Strong signals send the post to a larger pool and the loop repeats. Weak signals stop distribution, usually within the first hour or so.

This leads to three practical rules. The first seconds decide the test. Clear topic language (said, shown and written) helps the platform find the right test audience. Shares and saves beat likes because they show real value to someone.

## 2. Instagram

### Ranking signals
- **[Confirmed]** Adam Mosseri, head of Instagram, has named the three most important signals across surfaces as **watch time**, **sends per reach** (DM shares) and **likes per reach**.
- **[Confirmed]** Likes weigh relatively more for reaching *followers* (connected content). Sends weigh relatively more for reaching *non-followers* (unconnected content: Reels tab, Explore).
- **[Reported]** Watch time and replay rate replaced the old "3-second view" emphasis. Some analyses estimate sends carry several times the weight of likes for non-follower reach.
- **[Confirmed]** Users can view and adjust the topics shaping their recommendations ("Your Algorithm" controls, rolled out from December 2025). Clear topic signals matter even more, because users can now actively tune topics in or out.

### Originality
- **[Confirmed]** Accounts that mostly post other people's content without meaningful edits are not recommended to non-followers (no Explore, Reels tab or suggested posts). **[Reported]** As of April 2026 this covers photos and carousels too, not just Reels.
- **[Confirmed]** Visible watermarks from other apps (e.g., a TikTok logo) are deprioritized. Always export a clean file and upload it natively.
- Cropping, speeding up or adding a border does **not** make content original. Commentary, new footage, and real editing or transformation do.
- **[Reported]** Re-uploading a Reel you already posted gets detected as a near-duplicate and suppressed.

### Trial Reels
- **[Confirmed]** Trial Reels are shown to non-followers first, so you can test without spamming your followers' feed. **[Reported]** Eligible public accounts (around 1k+ followers) see results after about 24h, and a strong trial can be auto-shared to followers based on its first ~72h.
- Use them for **new** experiments (new hooks, formats or topics), not reposts of existing Reels.

### Hashtags, keywords and search
- **[Confirmed]** Since December 2025, hashtags are **capped at 5 per post/Reel**. Choose 3–5 specific ones (topic + niche + community). Generic tags (#love, #reels) do nothing.
- **[Reported]** Keywords in the caption carry more weight for search than hashtags. Write the caption in natural language that includes the words people would type ("easy high-protein breakfast", "Notion budget template").
- Put keywords in the **name field** of the profile (it's searchable), the bio, on-screen text, spoken audio, and alt text for images.

### Formats and when to use them
| Format | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels** | Reach to non-followers, growth | Highest reach rate of all formats [Reported]. Long Reels are allowed, but short wins discovery. |
| **Carousels** | Saves, depth, education, engagement per viewer | [Reported] Buffer's 52M-post analysis: carousels ~6.9% engagement by reach vs. Reels ~3.3%, while Reels get more total reach. Up to 20 slides. Carousels can resurface with a different first slide. |
| **Stories** | Relationship with existing followers, polls, links, BTS | Not a discovery surface. Good for converting warm audience (link stickers, DMs). |
| **Static photo** | Announcements, strong single images | Lowest reach. |
| **Broadcast channels / DMs** | Superfans, launches | Conversions; send-to-DM behavior is the most valuable signal |
| **Collab posts** | Borrowing another account's audience | The post shows on both profiles, and both audiences count. |

### Length
- **[Reported]** Reels can run far longer than 90s now, but a Reel's job is discovery and discovery favors short. For most content aim for **15–60s**, with 7–15s for pure entertainment/loops and 60–90s only when the story holds attention throughout.
- **[Reported]** Several analyses show 60–90s Reels getting the highest *average views* for creators who can sustain retention. Length is a result of how good the content is, not a lever you pull on its own.

### Profile grid
- **[Confirmed]** Since January 2025 the profile grid shows posts as **3:4 portrait tiles**. Design covers so the important part sits in the centre 3:4 area. Upload feed images at **1080×1350 (4:5)**, or **1080×1440 (3:4)**, which isn't cropped on the grid.

## 3. TikTok

### Ranking signals
- **[Confirmed]** TikTok says recommendations rely on user interactions (watch time, whether a video is finished, rewatches, likes, shares, comments, follows, "not interested"), video information (captions, sounds, hashtags) and device/account settings. Follower count is not a direct factor.
- **[Reported]** Watch time and completion are by far the heaviest signals. Several 2026 analyses describe a rising completion bar, with ~70% completion cited as what separates videos that keep expanding from those that stall. Shares and saves are weighted well above likes.
- **[Reported]** Testing happens in waves: a few hundred viewers first, measured over the first 30–90 minutes, then larger pools if signals clear.

### Search (TikTok SEO)
- Many users search TikTok like a search engine. Signals for search:
  - **Spoken keywords:** TikTok transcribes audio, so say the topic phrase out loud early.
  - **On-screen text:** it's indexed, so put the keyword phrase in the opening text card.
  - **Caption:** natural keyword sentence + 3–5 relevant hashtags.
- **[Reported]** Hashtag-driven discovery grew strongly in 2026. Hashtags still help TikTok categorize content.

### Length
- **[Reported]** 15–45s for most content. 20–35s is a common sweet spot for viral content. Longer (1–3 min+) works for storytelling, tutorials and series when retention holds. TikTok allows much longer uploads, but long only helps if people stay.

### Business accounts
- **[Confirmed]** Business accounts must use the **Commercial Music Library (CML)**, which has ~1M pre-cleared tracks. **The CML license covers TikTok only**, so you can't reuse the track when reposting to Instagram/YouTube.
- Spark Ads boost organic posts (yours or a creator's) as ads. Boost proven winners, not untested posts.
- TikTok Shop: product-tagged videos and LIVE. Short demos and UGC-style videos with a clear offer convert best.

### Culture
- Lo-fi, native, trend-aware and personality-led. Over-polished ad-like content signals "skip". Use native text, native features (stitch, duet, green screen, reply-to-comment video) and trending sounds, where licensing allows.

## 4. YouTube Shorts

### Ranking signals
- **[Confirmed]** Shorts analytics shows **"Viewed vs. swiped away"**: the share of people who watched rather than swiped past in the feed. This is the key early signal.
- **[Reported]** Benchmarks: ~70%+ viewed is strong, ~50/50 is average, under ~30–40% viewed and distribution tends to collapse. Retention near or above ~70% (and loops/rewatches) drives the biggest Shorts.
- **[Reported]** As with the others: small seed audience → expand or stop, with the decision often made in the first hour.

### Specs and features
- **[Confirmed]** Shorts can be up to **3 minutes** (for uploads since Oct 15, 2024), at 1:1 up to 9:16. The 3-minute limit includes any outro.
- **[Reported]** Under 60s still performs best for most topics. Use 60–180s for complete tutorials/stories.
- **[Confirmed]** You can attach a **related video** link to a Short. Use it to send Short viewers to a relevant long-form video, which is the main way Shorts grow a long-form channel.
- Titles and descriptions are searchable on YouTube and Google, so use a clear keyword phrase in the title. YouTube is the most "evergreen" short-form platform, and Shorts can get views for months.

### Culture
- Clear, fast, searchable, evergreen. Loops work well. Tutorials, explainers, facts, satisfying processes, and clips from long-form videos (re-edited for vertical, not just cropped).

## 5. LinkedIn

- **[Reported]** **Dwell time** (time spent on the post) is the dominant signal, above likes. Meaningful comments (not "Great post!") are strong signals.
- **[Reported]** Native formats get stronger distribution. Native video gets viewed longer and reaches more people than a YouTube link. Posts with external links are deprioritized, so put links in a comment or skip them.
- **[Reported]** Document/carousel posts lead engagement (~3.2%), then polls (~2.8%), native video (~2.4%) and text+image (~1.9%).
- **Video:** 30–90s, vertical (9:16) or square (1:1), **burned-in captions** (most viewers watch muted), and a text title card in the first frame saying what the video is about.
- **Text posts:** the first two lines are the hook, because that's what shows before "…see more". Use short paragraphs, white space, one insight and a question that invites expert replies.
- Tone: professional but human. Founder stories, lessons, frameworks, behind-the-scenes, contrarian industry takes. Overtly promotional posts underperform.

## 6. Facebook, Threads, X (brief)

- **Facebook:** Reels can be shared from Instagram via Meta's cross-posting. The audience skews older. Groups drive community. Treat it as distribution for content made elsewhere, unless the audience lives there.
- **Threads:** text-first and conversational. Good for testing hooks and opinions as text before turning them into videos.
- **X:** news, tech and commentary. Native video works, threads work for frameworks. Mainly useful for B2B/tech audiences.

## 8. Engagement benchmarks (and why they disagree)

Benchmarks depend on methodology. "Engagement rate" may be divided by followers or by reach, and computed per post or per account. Compare only like with like, and mostly compare the user against their own past posts.

| Platform | Typical figure | Source / method |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram (all formats) | ~0.45–0.55% per post | Socialinsider 2026, by followers |
| Instagram Reels | ~2.7% (vs. carousels ~1.4%) | Dash Social 2026, by followers |
| Instagram carousels vs. Reels | ~6.9% vs. ~3.3% | Buffer, by reach, 52M posts |
| TikTok | ~3.7–4.9% | Multiple 2026 reports, by views/followers |
| LinkedIn | docs ~3.2%, polls ~2.8%, video ~2.4%, text+image ~1.9% | 2026 aggregator data |
| YouTube Shorts | 70%+ viewed vs. swiped = strong | Third-party analyses |

**[Reported]** Instagram engagement per post fell ~17% year over year into 2026 as ranking shifted toward watch time. A falling like-rate doesn't automatically mean content got worse.
