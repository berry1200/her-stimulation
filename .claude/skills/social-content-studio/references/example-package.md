# Where your $14 sourdough actually goes

**Platform:** Instagram Reels (primary) · TikTok, YouTube Shorts variants
**Target length:** ~35s · **Format:** Behind the scenes + cost breakdown (talking head, process B-roll, receipt graphic)
**Pillar:** Behind the bakery · **Funnel:** TOFU → MOFU · **Goal metric:** sends per reach
**One-sentence idea:** This video shows bread buyers why a $14 sourdough loaf costs what it does, by walking through the real costs and 48 hours behind one loaf.
**Assumptions:** The cost figures are placeholders. Swap in the bakery's real numbers before filming (a cost claim has to be true). The baker is comfortable on camera, and the account is a business account, so no trending library music.

**Why people will share it:** It answers the most common objection at the stall ("$14 for bread?"). Fans send it to the friend who complains about the price, and other small-business owners share it because they recognise the problem. Transparency also builds trust (MOFU) while the reach comes from the shares (TOFU).

**Spoken word count:** 82 · **Estimated runtime:** ~30s of voice-over across a 35s cut (checked with `scripts/script_tools.py time`). The breathing room goes on the crunch, the steam reveal and the hold on the crumb.

## Edit notes
- **Pacing:** open on the crunch at frame 1 (no intro). Change visuals every 2–3s. The receipt graphic adds one line per spoken cost. Punch in 115% on "nobody sees".
- **Captions:** burned in, 2–4 words per chunk, white with a black outline. Highlight dollar amounts in the brand's wheat-gold accent. Keep them inside the safe zone (≈70% down).
- **Sound:** natural sound carries it (crunch, flour, oven door, paper bag). If adding music, use the Meta Sound Collection (business account), soft and acoustic, ducked well under the VO.
- **Ending:** cut hard after "$14 for bread?". The last line flows into "This loaf is $14…", which encourages a rewatch.
